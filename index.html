Biography

Robert Schwartz is a professional investor responsible for supporting and co-managing $4.5bn in fixed income assets. Robert specializes in portfolio construction across various credit sectors including government bonds, agency bonds, investment-grade corporates, mortgage-backed securities, high-yield corporates, and international and EMD. Prior to public credit, Robert worked in private wealth management at First Commonwealth Bank and in corporate finance at The PNC Financial Services Group. In his free time, Robert enjoys angel investing, focusing on early-stage high-growth companies that have the potential to both generate a strong ROIC and benefit society. Specifically, pre-seed, seed, and Series A stage companies within sectors like robotics, software, hardware, artificial intelligence, AgTech, MedTech, FinTech, cybersecurity, and real estate. Robert is on the board of 99 Tartans Alumni Angels, a 501(c)3 dedicated to supporting Carnegie Mellon University alumni entrepreneurs. Robert earned his MBA from Carnegie Mellon University and his undergraduate degree in Finance and Legal Studies from the Indiana University of Pennsylvania. He is a registered Investment Adviser Representative holding his FINRA Series 65 license. When time allows, Robert enjoys traveling, cooking, and the movies.
